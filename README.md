# Sum up 18 November's world news in one image — entry

`main.py` copies the bundled artwork `art18.jpg` to `/output/entry.png`.
Python 3.13, standard library only, no network, deterministic output.
Artwork: 1024x1024 JPEG, under 1 MiB, as required.

The artwork is a newspaper front page, "WORLD HEADLINE NEWS —
Wednesday, November 18, 2026". Its main story is the historic
trilateral meeting of Presidents Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin
at the APEC summit in Shenzhen. The side panels show the U.S. sinking
five Iranian tankers and Tehran striking back, Indonesia and China
signing a $71.8 billion strategic partnership witnessed in Shenzhen,
and the International Court of Justice ruling that Myanmar committed
genocide against the Rohingya.
